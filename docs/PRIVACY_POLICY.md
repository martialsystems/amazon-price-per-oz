### 5. Information the Extension processes

#### 5.1 Information processed locally in your browser

When you use the Extension on a supported Amazon domain, the Extension’s content script may **read information already present in the page** for the sole purpose of providing unit-price ranking, including without limitation:

- Product titles and visible listing text;
- Displayed prices and Amazon-shown unit prices (e.g., “$0.12/oz”);
- Structural page elements needed to identify search-result cards (e.g., product identifiers exposed in the page markup).

This processing occurs **on your device**, within the browser process. We do **not** transmit this information to our servers because the Extension **has no such servers** and makes **no network requests** initiated by the Extension for data collection, analytics, or advertising.

#### 5.2 Ephemeral interface state

The Extension may keep **transient, in-memory state** while your browser is running, such as:

- Whether the overlay is enabled or disabled (session toggle);
- Temporary position of a movable overlay within the current browsing session;
- Transient messaging between the Extension’s background script and tabs.

This state is **not** designed to be written as a permanent profile of your shopping activity. The Extension does **not** request the Chrome `storage` permission and does **not** use `chrome.storage` or long-lived `localStorage` for shopping data.

**Note:** Your browser or operating system may maintain its own caches, history, or crash logs outside our control.

#### 5.3 Information we do not collect

We do not intentionally collect, upload, or maintain:

- Names, email addresses, or contact details (except if you email us for support);
- Precise geolocation;
- Authentication credentials, cookies for the purpose of account takeover, or payment card data;
- A persistent log of products you viewed or prices you compared;
- Advertising identifiers for cross-site tracking;
- Data from websites other than the Amazon host permissions declared in the Extension manifest.

#### 5.4 Information you provide voluntarily

If you contact us by email, we will process the content of your message and your email address to respond. We retain such correspondence only as long as reasonably necessary for support, legal compliance, or dispute resolution.

### 6. Permissions and host access

The Extension declares **host permissions** only for specific Amazon domains (for example, `*.amazon.com` and certain other regional Amazon sites listed in the manifest). These permissions allow the Extension to run scripts on those pages so it can read visible listing data and modify the display of search results **in your browser**.

The Extension does **not** request broad permissions such as “read all website data,” identity, geolocation, or unlimited storage.

Chrome may display permission warnings based on the manifest. Those warnings describe technical capability, not an intent to exfiltrate data.

### 7. How we use information

Locally processed page content is used solely to:

1. Estimate or read price-per-unit values;
2. Rank, label, or reorder search results on the page at your request (e.g., “Sort Now”);
3. Display the Extension’s user interface (overlay, badges, toolbar badge).

We do not use Extension-processed data for advertising, profiling, resale, or training of machine-learning models.
