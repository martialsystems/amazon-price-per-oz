# Privacy Policy

**Unit Price Sort for Amazon Search**  
**Martial Systems LLC** · martialsys@gmail.com · https://martialsys.net/  
**Effective:** August 4, 2026

We do **not** collect, store, sell, or send your shopping data anywhere.

The extension only reads product titles, prices, and unit-price text **already shown on Amazon pages in your browser**, so it can rank listings by price per unit. That work stays on your device.

Enable/disable and overlay position are temporary browser UI state only (not a shopping history). The optional **Keep Alexa off** setting stores a single on/off preference in the browser (no product, search, or account data).

We do not use ads, analytics SDKs, or developer backends for this extension.

If you email us, we use your message only to reply.

Not affiliated with Amazon.com, Inc.
