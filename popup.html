Unit Price Sort

Pin the extension, then click the icon to show/hide the overlay.

  • On search pages: Sort Now ranks by $/unit
  • Overlay Disable = same as toolbar OFF
  • Badge shows OFF when disabled
  • Right-click icon → Keep Alexa off (checkbox)
Only sorts when a unit price exists (oz, fl oz, sheets, count). Toilet paper ranks by price per sheet. For single items like a coffee maker, use Amazon’s Sort by: Price · Low to High.

No popup on icon click (toggle only). Nothing shopping-related is saved.
